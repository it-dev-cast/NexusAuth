Nexus One

One identity for every module on the platform.

The Authentication Service issues every login and every permission for Nexus One — JWT-based, role-aware, and fully audited from day one. No module ships its own login screen or user table.

  • HS256-signed access & refresh tokens, with rotation on every refresh
  • Role-based access enforced identically at every API endpoint
  • Every action — success or denial — written to an immutable audit trail

SecurityEvents

Events

A durable stream of everything the audit log records.

Event stream

Every event mirrors an audit row 1:1 — it fires automatically whenever an action is audit-logged (see app/services/audit_service.py). Read-only by design; the only writer is the app itself. Nothing consumes this stream yet (no workflow engine is wired up) — this is the durable record ready for one later.

Assets & DataMaster Management

Master Management

Every master list in one place — choose one to manage.

Master Management reports →

Department master

* Required

Shared, company-wide list used by the Employee form's Department dropdown. Deactivating one here removes it from that dropdown without affecting employees already assigned to it.

Job role master

* Required

Shared, company-wide list that fills the Employee form's Job role dropdown (active roles only, so a change here shows up there straight away). Not the same as a user's RBAC role (Admin/Operator/Read-only) on the Users page — this is a job title. Deactivating one here removes it from that dropdown without affecting employees already assigned to it.

Application Master

Register the applications your teams use and manage which employees have access to each. This records who is meant to use an application — it does not sign anyone in to, or create accounts in, the application itself.

Add Application

* Required

Deactivating an application stops it being given to anyone new and removes it from the Employee form. People who already have it keep it (shown as inactive) until you revoke it, and reactivating restores it as it was.

Manufacturer master

* Required

Shared list used by the Asset form's Manufacturer dropdown. Deactivating one here removes it from that dropdown without affecting assets already registered under it.

Model master

* Required

Shared list used by the Asset form's Model dropdown, independent of Manufacturer (picking one does not filter the other). Deactivating one here removes it from that dropdown without affecting assets already registered under it.
